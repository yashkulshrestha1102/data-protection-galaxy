## 🚨 1. Purpose

This document sets out the internal procedure that Businezexcellence StartX LLP ("the Company") will follow upon becoming aware of a **personal data breach** while operating the Legal Galaxy platform (https://dpdpa.businezexcellence.com).

A "personal data breach" means any unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss of access to, or destruction of personal data.

This plan ensures compliance with:
- **Section 8(6)** of the DPDP Act 2023 — obligation to notify the Data Protection Board of India (DPB) and affected Data Principals.
- **Rule 7** of the DPDP Rules, 2025 — timelines and format of breach notification (notification to DPB within **72 hours** of becoming aware).

## ⏱️ 3. Breach Response Timeline

| Time | Action | Responsible |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **T + 0 hours** | Breach detected/suspected. **Stop the leak** (revoke access, patch vulnerability). Log initial facts. | Whoever detects |
| **T + 1 hour** | Incident Lead informed. Response Team convened. Incident Register opened. | Detector → Incident Lead |
| **T + 6 hours** | Containment completed. Preliminary impact assessment (what data, how many Data Principals, severity). | Technical Lead |
| **T + 24 hours** | Detailed investigation. Determine if DPB notification is required (see Section 4). Draft DPB notification. | Incident Lead + Legal |
| **T + 48 hours** | Legal review of DPB notification. Notify Data Principals if high risk. | Legal + Comms |
| **T + 72 hours (MAX)** | **File notification with Data Protection Board of India** via dpb.gov.in (or as prescribed). | Incident Lead |
| **T + 7 days** | Post-incident review. Update security measures. Document lessons learned. | Incident Lead |

## 🎯 4. When to Notify the DPB

**Notification to the DPB is MANDATORY** for any breach that meets the threshold under Rule 7 of DPDP Rules 2025:

- Any breach affecting personal data that could cause harm to Data Principals.
- Even if the breach is contained, if personal data was accessed, altered, or lost, notify.
- Examples: unauthorised access to Google Sheet (lead data), compromised email account, exposed API keys, hacked AWS/storage.

**When in doubt — NOTIFY.** The cost of over-notification is far lower than non-notification (₹200 crore penalty).

---

## 📋 5. What Must Be Included in the DPB Notification

As per Rule 7, the notification to the DPB must include:

1. **Description of the breach** — nature, circumstances, date and time of detection.
2. **Categories and approximate number of Data Principals affected.**
3. **Categories and approximate volume of personal data records affected.**
4. **Likely consequences** of the breach for Data Principals.
5. **Measures taken or proposed** to address the breach and mitigate adverse effects.
6. **Contact details** of the Data Protection Officer / Grievance Officer.
7. **Any other information** the DPB may require.

**Format:** As prescribed by the DPB (currently via online portal or email to the Board).

---

## 📢 6. Notifying Affected Data Principals

If the breach poses a **high risk** to Data Principals, notify them **without delay** via:

1. **Email** to the affected email address on record.
2. **Website notice** on https://dpdpa.businezexcellence.com (if mass notification required).

The notification must include:
- Nature of the breach.
- Data affected.
- Consequences likely to occur.
- Measures taken / proposed.
- Steps the Data Principal should take to protect themselves.
- Contact details for queries.

## 🛠️ 8. Post-Incident Review

Within **7 days** of closing the incident:

1. Conduct a root cause analysis (RCA).
2. Update security measures, access controls, or vendor agreements.
3. Update this Breach Response Plan if gaps identified.
4. Train team on lessons learned.
